# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, students, and data practitioners exploring the openSenseMap archive. They use the interface to find stations, inspect readings on maps and charts, run filtered analyses, and export data.

## Product Purpose

OSeM Archive makes historical openSenseMap station data browsable and exportable. Success means users can quickly understand archive coverage, narrow the dataset, and retrieve the data they need without guessing what the backend contains.

## Brand Personality

Clear, technical, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style hero layouts, decorative dashboards, oversized metric cards, and visual treatments that slow down dense data exploration.

## Design Principles

- Keep archive coverage and data scope visible.
- Favor familiar product controls over novelty.
- Preserve map-first exploration and fast scanning.
- Use restrained emphasis for status, filters, and selected data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
